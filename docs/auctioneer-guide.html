Auctioneer's guide: how the page works and how to run the auction

How the page works

The whole auction is one file, index.html. There is no server and nothing to install. Open it in Chrome or Edge on the auctioneer's laptop. Every action saves automatically in that browser, so the auction comes back after a refresh or a crash.

Before the event

  1. Setup → Event & teams: enter the event name and the teams: name, short code, colour and owner for each.
  2. Setup → Auction rules: set the purse, squad size, overseas limit, RTM cards and bid steps.
  3. Setup → Players: load the IPL players or import your own CSV. Then press SAVE PHOTOS OFFLINE while you are online, so photos work without internet at the venue.
  4. Setup → Sets & order: choose random order (the default), random within each set, or fixed set order.
  5. Setup → Surprises: choose how often mystery and wild card players pop in, and how many of each.
  6. The sample practice auction is still loaded. Clear it with Setup → Backup & reset → Clear results.
  7. Export backup before the event and again at every break.
  8. Projector: press V to show the projector view on the same screen, or open a second window and drag it onto the projector.

Running the auction

1. StartSTART AUCTION or Enter
2. OpenEnter or tap the first team
3. BidTap a team or press its number
4. CloseG once, twice, close
5. ResultSold or unsold, next player comes up
  1. Start: press START AUCTION or Enter. The projector shows "THE AUCTION IS LIVE" and the first random player comes onto the block.
  2. Open the bidding: press Enter, or just tap the team that bids first. The opening bid is the player's base price.
  3. Take bids: when a paddle goes up, tap that team's button or press its number key (1–9, 0, Shift+1–5).
    • Space brings the other team back in during a two-team bidding war.
    • The + buttons raise the step; C enters a custom amount.
    • The page blocks any bid a team can't afford or isn't allowed to make.
  4. Close the bidding: click the BIDDING box or press G. The first press calls Going once, the second Going twice, the third closes it. Any new bid resets it to OPEN.
  5. Result: with a bid, it confirms the sale (offering RTM first if a team holds a card for that player). With no bids, it asks whether to mark the player unsold. The next player then comes up automatically.

Surprise players: mystery and wild card

  • They pop in automatically every few players. The dice box in the control room, which only you can see, shows who comes next.
  • Click the dice box to:
    • pick a different surprise player;
    • turn any available player into a mystery or wild card;
    • pop one in now;
    • swap the surprise player on the block, as long as nobody has bid yet.
  • Mystery players show clues on their own and are then revealed. M shows the next clue and Shift+M reveals the player now.

Tips for the auctioneer

Keep one hand on the number keys and the other on G and S. Call "going once" and "going twice" out loud as you press G.
  • Holding a key down or double-clicking never counts twice.
  • Mistakes are fixable: R undoes, and the Fixes page handles later corrections. Nothing is ever deleted from the history.
  • Photos need internet unless you pressed SAVE PHOTOS OFFLINE beforehand. Players without a photo show their initials.
  • Export a backup at every break. Restore backup loads it on any machine.
  • At the end, Reports has team squads, statistics and awards, with CSV, Excel and PDF exports.

Quick checklist on the day

Before you start

  • Sample data cleared
  • Teams, purses and rules checked
  • Photos saved offline
  • Backup exported
  • Projector showing "starts soon"

During and after

  • One person on the laptop, one calling bids
  • Backup at every break
  • Check squads in Reports
  • Show awards on the projector
  • Export final results

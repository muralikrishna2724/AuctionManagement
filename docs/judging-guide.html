Every team ends with exactly 7 players in the same shape (1 wicketkeeper, 2 batters, 2 all-rounders, 2 bowlers), so judges don't check whether a team has the right roles, but how good, how well balanced and how well bought its 7 players are. Four criteria (60 points) are worked out by the app from the squads, the same for every judge; each judge scores the other five (40 points) in steps of 0.5.

The nine criteria

💰 Value for Money
🤖 worked out by the app		20High-rated players for low money. The app works out the squad's fair price: what players of those ratings cost in this auction (from the prices actually paid; a higher rating is worth more). Paying exactly the fair price = 10; every 5% below it adds 1 point, every 5% above takes 1 away.
paid 50% below fair → 20 · 25% below → 15 · fair → 10 · 25% above → 5 · 50% above → 0. Unspent purse only counts through this.
⚖️ Balanced Team Combo
🤖 worked out by the app		10Are the keeper, the batters, the all-rounders and the bowlers all equally strong? The app compares the average rating of each role group: the gap between the strongest and the weakest group.
gap 5 or less → 10 · 10 → 8 · 15 → 6 · 20 → 4 · 25 → 2 · 30+ → 0

How the winning team is decided

Where to find the facts: the Judging page fills in the four 🤖 criteria for every team by itself. Reports has each team's squad, prices, spend and a Team analysis; the hints under the judges' boxes come from the ratings and prices. They are a guide: the judges decide their 40 points.
1. After the hammerThe auction ends when every squad has its 7 players. Open Reports on the projector or a laptop.
2. Team pitch (optional)Each team owner gets 1 minute to explain their squad and plan.
3. Score aloneEach judge scores every team on their 5 criteria, without discussing scores first.
4. Enter the scoresOn the Judging page, each judge has a tab; type in the 5 judged scores. The 4 automatic ones are already there.
5. StandingsThe app averages the judges who scored. The highest average wins.

The result

  • Winner – 🏆 Auction Champions: the team with the highest average total out of 100 across all judges. Second place is the runner-up.
  • All judges score all teams. The standings average whichever judges have scored so far and show which teams still have missing scores, so wait until every score is in before announcing the winner.
  • Use the same scale for every team. If a judge sees they were too harsh or generous on the first team, adjust before the scores are final, not after the standings appear.
  • Scores are final once the head judge announces them. Show the winner on the projector with 📣 SHOW ON SCREEN under 🏆 Auction Champions.

If two teams tie on the average

  1. More points for ⭐ Average Player Rating.
  2. Still tied: more points for 💰 Value for Money.
  3. Still tied: higher judges' average for 💪 Team Strength & Match Winners.
  4. Still tied: the judges vote between the tied teams; the head judge has the casting vote.

Rules for judges

  • Judge the squad, not the owners' bidding drama, team spirit or how loud the supporters were.
  • A judge who is linked to a team (friend, classmate, owner) should say so before scoring; the head judge can ask them to skip that team, and the app then averages the other judges.
  • Judge every team against the same players: the IPL ratings and records shown in the app, not personal favourites.
  • Mystery players and wild cards count like any other player once bought.

Fun awards (separate from the score)

These don't change the winner. The Judging page suggests a team for each from the auction stats; the judges can accept the suggestion or pick another.

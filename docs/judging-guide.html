Every team ends with exactly 7 players in the same shape (1 wicketkeeper, 2 batters, 2 all-rounders, 2 bowlers). Each team is scored out of 100 on four criteria of 25 points each. All four are worked out by the app from the squads, using the player ratings, batting hands, bowling styles and the prices paid, so every team is measured the same way and the scores are ready the moment the auction ends.

The four criteria

CriterionPointsHow the app scores it
⭐ Average Player Rating25How good the 7 players are. The average rating of the squad, minus 70.
95+ → 25 · 90 → 20 · 88 → 18 · 85 → 15 · 82 → 12 · 80 → 10 · 75 → 5 · 70 or less → 0
💰 Value for Money25High-rated players for low money. The app works out the squad's fair price: what players of those ratings cost in this auction (from the prices actually paid; a higher rating is worth more). Paying exactly the fair price = 12.5; every 4% below it adds 1 point, every 4% above takes 1 away.
paid 50% below fair → 25 · 25% below → 19 · fair → 12.5 · 25% above → 6.5 · 50% above → 0. Unspent purse only counts through this.
⚖️ Balanced Team Combo25Are the keeper, the batters, the all-rounders and the bowlers all equally strong? The app takes the average rating of each role group and the gap between the strongest and the weakest group: within 5 = 25, then 1 point off for every rating point more.
gap 5 or less → 25 · 10 → 20 · 15 → 15 · 20 → 10 · 25 → 5 · 30+ → 0
🔀 Batting–Bowling Combination25Three parts:
  • Left- and right-hand batters among the 5 batting options (keeper, batters, all-rounders): 2 or more of each = 10, only 1 of one hand = 5, all the same = 0.
  • Pace and spin among the 4 bowling options (bowlers, all-rounders): 2 or more of each = 10, only 1 of one type = 5, all the same = 0.
  • Batting as strong as bowling: 5, minus 1 for every 2 points between the app's batting and bowling strength bars.

How the winning team is decided

1. During the auctionThe Judging page keeps every team's four scores up to date as players are bought.
2. After the hammerWhen every squad has its 7 players, the status reads SQUAD COMPLETE and the scores are final.
3. Judges checkThe judges look over the squads and the scores (Reports has each squad, its prices and spend).
4. TiesSettled by the rules below; the judges only step in if it is still level.
5. AnnounceThe team on top is the 🏆 Auction Champions: show it with 📣 SHOW ON SCREEN.

The result

  • Winner – 🏆 Auction Champions: the team with the highest total out of 100. Second place is the runner-up.
  • The scores come only from the squads (ratings, hands, bowling styles, prices), so the bidding drama, team spirit or the loudest supporters don't change them.
  • Mystery players and wild cards count like any other player once bought.
  • If a player's details are wrong in the list (for example a missing batting hand), fix it on the Players page before the results are announced; the scores update by themselves.

If two teams tie on the total

  1. More points for ⭐ Average Player Rating.
  2. Still tied: more points for 💰 Value for Money.
  3. Still tied: more points for ⚖️ Balanced Team Combo, then 🔀 Batting–Bowling Combination.
  4. Still tied: the judges vote between the tied teams; the head judge has the casting vote.

The Judging page already lists tied teams in this order.

What the judges do

  • Check the squads and the scores before they are announced, and settle any tie that the rules above leave level.
  • Pick the fun awards (below). The app suggests a team for each from the auction stats; the judges can accept it or choose another.
  • Optional: give each team owner 1 minute to present their squad before the results. It doesn't change the score, but it's a good show.

Every team ends with exactly 7 players in the same shape (1 wicketkeeper, 2 batters, 2 all-rounders, 2 bowlers), so judges don't check whether a team has the right roles, but how good, how well balanced and how well bought its 7 players are. Each judge scores each team out of 100 on the seven criteria below, in steps of 0.5.

The seven criteria

CriterionPointsWhat to look at
⭐ Overall Team Strength25The quality of all 7 players: ratings, IPL records, proven match winners and stars. A squad with no weak link beats one with two stars and five fillers.
22–25 nearly all 7 are top-class · 16–21 strong core, one or two average picks · 10–15 a mix of good and weak · 0–9 mostly weak players
⚖️ Team Balance20Every squad has the same roles, so judge the balance inside them: batting and bowling about equally strong; left- and right-hand batters; pace and spin among the 4 bowling options (2 bowlers + 2 all-rounders); overseas players used where they add most.
17–20 no gaps at all · 12–16 one small gap (e.g. no spinner) · 6–11 clearly lopsided · 0–5 badly unbalanced
🔄 Versatility15Players who do more than one job: genuine all-rounders who bat and bowl, a keeper who opens or finishes, bowlers who can bat, players tagged 🔄 Versatile. More options = more ways to win.
13–15 several multi-skill players · 9–12 the 2 all-rounders deliver · 4–8 little cover · 0–3 one-dimensional

Where to find the facts: the app's Reports page has each team's squad, prices, spend and a Team analysis with a bar for every criterion. The bars and the hints on the Judging page come from the player ratings and auction prices. They are a guide, not a verdict: the judges decide the scores.

How the winning team is decided

1. After the hammerThe auction ends when every squad has its 7 players. Open Reports on the projector or a laptop.
2. Team pitch (optional)Each team owner gets 1 minute to explain their squad and plan.
3. Score aloneEach judge scores every team on the 7 criteria, without discussing scores first.
4. Enter the scoresOn the Judging page, each judge has a tab; type the scores in. Totals are added up for you.
5. StandingsThe app averages the judges who scored. The highest average wins.

The result

  • Winner – 🏆 Auction Champions: the team with the highest average total out of 100 across all judges. Second place is the runner-up.
  • All judges score all teams. The standings average whichever judges have scored so far and show which teams still have missing scores, so wait until every score is in before announcing the winner.
  • Use the same scale for every team. If a judge sees they were too harsh or generous on the first team, adjust before the scores are final, not after the standings appear.
  • Scores are final once the head judge announces them. Show the winner on the projector with 📣 SHOW ON SCREEN under 🏆 Auction Champions.

If two teams tie on the average

  1. Higher average for ⭐ Overall Team Strength.
  2. Still tied: higher average for ⚖️ Team Balance.
  3. Still tied: higher average for 💰 Value for Money.
  4. Still tied: the judges vote between the tied teams; the head judge has the casting vote.

Rules for judges

  • Judge the squad, not the owners' bidding drama, team spirit or how loud the supporters were.
  • A judge who is linked to a team (friend, classmate, owner) should say so before scoring; the head judge can ask them to skip that team, and the app then averages the other judges.
  • Judge every team against the same players: the IPL ratings and records shown in the app, not personal favourites.
  • Mystery players and wild cards count like any other player once bought.

Fun awards (separate from the score)

These don't change the winner. The Judging page suggests a team for each from the auction stats; the judges can accept the suggestion or pick another.
